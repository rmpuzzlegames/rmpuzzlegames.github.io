Tangle Worlds - Delete your account and data

Tangle Worlds by RM Puzzle Games (Android, com.rmpuzzlegames.tangleworlds)

You only have a Tangle Worlds account if you signed in with Google for cloud save. You can delete that account and everything stored with it at any time.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Tangle Worlds and tap Settings (the gear).
  2. Tap Cloud save.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your cloud copy and your account are deleted straight away. Progress on your phone stays until you reset it (Settings › Reset progress) or uninstall the game.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the game, email rmpuzzlegames.support@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Tangle Worlds - delete my account". We delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • your Tangle Worlds account (Google account user ID, email address, name and profile picture link received at sign-in);
  • your cloud copy of game progress (levels, stars, coins, worlds, Style items, streaks), its save times and app version;
  • your "News by email" choice; we stop sending you emails.
  • your leaderboard lines (nickname, levels, character and hat), your friend code, your friendships and any waiting friend requests.

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion. Anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports, which are not linked to your account, are kept as described in the privacy policy (analytics data for 2 months, crash reports for 90 days).
