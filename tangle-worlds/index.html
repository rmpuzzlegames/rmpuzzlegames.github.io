Features

Simple to learn, hard to master

Every critter can only move where its head points. Plan your moves, clear the way and untangle boards of hundreds of critters.

Characters and style

Turn your critters into a magic broom, a toy train, a champion swimmer and more. Collect hats, glasses, trails and victory parties, and try them on before you buy them with coins.

Hidden pictures

Every few levels hides a picture: a fox, an owl, a rocket and many more. Clear the board to reveal it.

Your own gallery

Every revealed picture hangs in your gallery. Share your favourites with friends.

Levels that never end

Hand-made tutorials, then endless boards in hearts, stars, rings and more, with easy breather levels along the way.

Daily Challenge

A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going for bigger rewards.

Worlds to unlock

Classic snakes, Jungle caterpillars, Robo worms, Ocean eels, Fantasy dragons and a Rangoli Festival, each with its own music.

Characters with personality

They blink, bump into each other, rear up and react with comic one-liners.

Stars, coins and gifts

Earn up to 3 stars per level, collect a daily gift and spend coins on hints when you are stuck.

Made to relax

Calm music, smooth animations, pinch to zoom and full offline play. No account needed.

Support

Questions and answers

Can't find your answer? Write to rmpuzzlegames.support@gmail.com and we will help.

When can I play Tangle Worlds?

Tangle Worlds is in its final testing and is coming soon to Google Play for Android phones. Follow us on YouTube to hear when it launches.

Is Tangle Worlds free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. You can buy optional coin packs and a one-time "Remove ads", and you can watch optional short videos for rewards such as a free hint or an extra heart. You never need to pay to keep playing.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Every level can be played offline. The internet is only used for ads and purchases.

I bought something but didn't get it

Purchases are delivered automatically. Make sure you are online and reopen the game: it checks with Google Play and delivers anything that was paid for. If it still doesn't show up, email us with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.).

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is stored only on your phone. Use Settings › Reset progress in the game, uninstall it, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. See the privacy policy for details.
