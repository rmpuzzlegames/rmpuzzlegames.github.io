Light Rush - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026
Publisher: RM Puzzle Games, India · Contact: rmpuzzlegames.support@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Light Rush: Festival Lights ("the Game") handles information. The Game is published by RM Puzzle Games ("we", "us", "our").

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one for a hint) and, from level 11 on, an occasional full-screen ad between levels. Players who buy "Remove ads" see no ads between levels.
  • Google AdMob may collect device information, including your device's advertising ID, as described in Section 4. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first.
  • Optional in-app purchases (a hint pack and "Remove ads") are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect analytics or crash reports.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following in its private storage on your device so that you can continue where you left off: unlocked levels, stars, the current level, hints, the daily-puzzle streak, whether "Remove ads" was bought, and settings (sound, vibration and reduced effects). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, your ad-consent choice is stored too. This information is not sent to us and contains no name, email address, phone number or contact details.

Android backup. If backup is on, Android may include the Game's data in your device backup to your Google Account. This backup is provided and controlled by Google, not by us.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. The Game's own code sends no data to any server operated by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob (Google LLC). When the Game starts and when ads are loaded or shown, Google's ad software may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and its ad-personalisation setting;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and network type;
  • approximate location (country), derived from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with, used for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit repetition, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show personalised ads. See How Google uses information in advertising and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change it at any time in the Game: Pause › Privacy options.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most devices). The Game is fully playable without watching any rewarded video.

5. In-app purchases

The Game offers an optional hint pack and "Remove ads", bought through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled only by Google; we never see or store them. Google tells the Game which item was bought and an order ID, which the Game keeps on your device so a purchase is delivered exactly once. "Remove ads" is restored automatically after a reinstall on the same Google account. Refunds: see Google Play's refund policy.

6. Technical data from the game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may automatically receive limited technical information about your device (such as device model, operating system and hardware capabilities). See the Unity Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We tell Google that the Game is not child-directed. If you believe a child under 13 is using the Game, contact us and we will help.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it: uninstall the Game, or use Settings › Apps › Light Rush › Storage › Clear storage. We hold no copy of your data. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal information, most requests concern data held by Google; we are happy to help you contact them.
