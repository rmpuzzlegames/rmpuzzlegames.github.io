Light Rush - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026
Publisher: RM Puzzle Games, India · Contact: rmpuzzlegames.support@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Light Rush: Festival Lights ("the Game") handles information. The Game is published by RM Puzzle Games ("we", "us", "our").

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. We do not run servers that receive or store your data.
  • We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following in its private storage on your device so that you can continue where you left off: unlocked levels, stars earned, the current level, and settings (sound, vibration and reduced effects). This information is not sent to us and contains no name, email address, phone number or contact details.

Android backup. If you have enabled backup on your Android device, Android may include the Game's saved data in your device backup to your Google Account. This backup is provided and controlled by Google, not by us.

3. Information we collect

None. The Game's own code does not send any data to us or to any third party.

4. Technical data from the game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may automatically receive limited technical information about your device (such as device model, operating system and hardware capabilities) to support and improve its engine. See the Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Device permissions

PermissionWhy
InternetIncluded by the Unity engine. The Game itself works fully offline and does not use the network for gameplay.
VibrationOptional haptic feedback; can be turned off in the Game's pause menu.

The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location.

6. Children

The Game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the Game or by using Settings › Apps › Light Rush › Storage › Clear storage on your device. We hold no copy of your data.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that uses data (for example ads or cloud save), we will update this policy at the same address before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
