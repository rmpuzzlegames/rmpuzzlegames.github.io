Chroma Crowd: Color Shooter - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Last updated: 18 November 2026
Publisher: RM Puzzle Games, India · Contact: rmpuzzlegames.support@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Chroma Crowd: Color Shooter ("the Game", package com.rmpuzzlegames.colorcrowd) handles information. The Game is published by RM Puzzle Games ("we", "us", "our").

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one, for example for double coins or a revive) and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. No ads are shown during a battle.
  • The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard), so we can improve it.
  • Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase may collect device information, including your device's advertising ID, as described in Sections 4 and 5. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first.
  • We do not run our own servers and we do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following in its private storage on your device so that you can continue where you left off: the current level and castle, coins, upgrades, unlocked heroes, skins and powers, kingdom buildings, the daily-reward streak and daily-mission progress, the story chapters you have seen, and settings (sound, music, vibration and effects). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, your ad-consent choice is stored too. This information is not sent to us and contains no name, email address, phone number or contact details.

Android backup. If backup is on, Android may include the Game's data in your device backup to your Google Account. This backup is provided and controlled by Google, not by us.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. The Game's own code sends no data to any server operated by us. The services in Sections 4 and 5 receive data on our behalf or for their own purposes as described there.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob (Google LLC). When the Game starts and when ads are loaded or shown, Google's ad software may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and its ad-personalisation setting;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and network type;
  • approximate location (country), derived from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with, used for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit repetition, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show personalised ads. See How Google uses information in advertising and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change it at any time in the Game: Settings › Privacy options.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most devices). The Game is fully playable without watching any rewarded video.

5. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the Game is played and to improve it, the Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC). It records game events such as levels started and finished (with the level number, result, time taken and the reason a level was lost), upgrades bought with in-game coins, rewarded and between-level ads shown, daily rewards and missions claimed, and tutorial progress. Together with these events Firebase processes an app-instance identifier, your advertising ID (where allowed), device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

We look at this information only in aggregated reports. We do not use it to identify you. See Privacy and Security in Firebase. Analytics data is kept according to the Firebase retention setting (by default up to 14 months) and then deleted.

6. Technical data from the game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may automatically receive limited technical information about your device (such as device model, operating system and hardware capabilities). See the Unity Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We tell Google that the Game is not child-directed. If you believe a child under 13 is using the Game, contact us and we will help.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it: uninstall the Game, or use Settings › Apps › Chroma Crowd › Storage › Clear storage. We hold no copy of your game data. Google keeps advertising and analytics data according to its own policies and the retention period in Section 5; you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal information, most requests concern data held by Google; write to us and we will help, including with deleting analytics data linked to your app instance.
