TangleWORLDS

A casual untangle puzzle with hidden pictures, worlds to unlock and characters to collect.

Tap a critter and it slides out head first, but only if nothing is in its way. Find the right order, clear the board and a hidden picture appears. Every level is generated and verified solvable.

  • Free to play

The game

Why players come back

Satisfying core loop

Endless, guaranteed-solvable levels with 48 board shapes and up to 350 critters per board. Difficulty rises step by step, with an easier level after every harder one.

Hidden pictures

Every fifth level hides a pixel-art picture that develops as the board is cleared, collected in a gallery of 30 pictures.

Seven worlds

Snakes, caterpillars, robo worms, eels, baby dragons and more: each world has its own critters, moves, reactions, music and look.

Characters and style

8 playable characters plus hats, glasses, trails, burrow looks and victory parties. Rarities, collection sets and a daily deal. Players try items on before they buy.

A living home screen

The player's character dances to a dhol beat on a lit stage and shows its best move when tapped, with today's deal and the next goal beside it.

Daily habits

Daily Challenge with streaks, a 7-day gift, daily missions, achievements and a first-play tutorial.

Publishing readiness

Built to be tested and scaled

Technology

  • Unity 6, Android (Google Play, AAB)
  • Procedural level generator with a solver and validator
  • Automatic quality for slower phones
  • Offline play, progress saved on the device

Monetization

  • Rewarded ads (hints, extra heart, double coins, free coins)
  • Interstitials with pacing rules
  • IAP: coin packs and remove-ads (Google Play Billing)
  • Coin sinks: Style shop, hints, continues

Data and live tuning

  • Firebase Analytics: level funnel, purchases, ads, Style shop and Home events
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Remote Config: prices, rewards, deals and ad pacing without an update
  • Consent flow for ads (UMP)
Status

Testing status and build access

Currently in Google Play Closed Testing. A playable Android build is available to publishing partners on request: send us the Google account emails of your team and we will invite them to the closed test. Retention and engagement numbers from the test are available on request.
