Short description

Match riders to their bus and clear the traffic jam in 1000 colourful puzzles.

About the game

In Color Transit, a crowd of riders is waiting at the stop and the parking lot is jammed with colourful buses. Tap a bus to drive it out to a loading bay, and every rider of the same colour hops aboard. Fill it up and off it goes! But plan carefully: bays are limited, and if every bay is full with nobody able to board, the route is stuck.

Relaxing to start and satisfying to master, every stop is a small puzzle about order and space: which bus goes first, and which one has to wait?

Features

  • 1000 levels across 20 city districts, on a city journey map
  • Daily Rush challenge every day and Rush Hour stops with bonus coins
  • Boosters: Hint, Undo and Extra Bay
  • Star chests, route chests and a 7-day daily reward streak
  • City themes: Sunny Park, Sunset Boulevard and Neon Night
  • A garage of buses, city cars, trucks and autos to unlock
  • Colour-blind friendly shape symbols
  • Plays offline, no timers, portrait or landscape
