Support

Questions and answers

Can't find your answer? Write to rmpuzzlegames.support@gmail.com and we will help.

Is Color Transit free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. You can buy optional coin packs and bundles, and you can watch optional short videos for extra rewards such as double coins or a free hint. You never need to pay or watch a video to keep playing.

I bought something but didn't get it

Purchases are delivered automatically. If one didn't arrive, make sure you are online and reopen the game: it checks with Google Play and delivers anything that was paid for. If it still doesn't show up, email us with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.) and we will sort it out.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Turn on Cloud Save in the game's Settings while signed in to Google Play Games. Your levels, stars, coins and boosters are saved to your Google account, so you can continue on another phone signed in to the same account.

Does the game need the internet?

No. You can play every level offline. The internet is only needed for optional videos, purchases and Cloud Save.

I find some colours hard to tell apart

Open Settings and turn on colour symbols. Every colour then also gets its own shape on the riders and buses.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is stored on your phone. Uninstall the game, or go to your phone's Settings › Apps › Color Transit › Storage › Clear storage. Cloud Save data can be removed from your Google account in the Play Games app. See the privacy policy for details.
